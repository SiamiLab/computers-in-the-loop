# Credits and sources

This repository accompanies the lecture notes **Computers in the Loop: Digital Control from
Sampling to Networked Agents**, prepared by Milad Siami (Northeastern University) for the
graduate course EECE 5610 Digital Control Systems.

- **Lecture notes of Prof. Mario Sznaier.** Chapters 1–11 of the notes are based on his
  handwritten lecture notes. The notebooks for those chapters recompute the notes' examples.
- **Course textbook.** C. L. Phillips, H. T. Nagle and A. Chakrabortty, *Digital Control System
  Analysis and Design*, 4th ed., Pearson, 2015. Several worked examples in Chapters 6–11 of the
  notes are adapted from it, and each is marked "Adapted from" there. The notebooks compute those
  examples from their models; no text or figures of the textbook are reproduced here.
  Two activities use related setups: activity 1 has the nominal dynamics of the textbook's
  Example 6.1, and activity 6 uses the frequencies of its Problem 3.7-7 for a different question.
- **Chapter 16** is based on Y.-M. Huang, A. C. B. de Oliveira, D. Murugan and M. Siami,
  "Balancing Agility and Communication: Denser Networks Require Faster Agents," *IEEE Open
  Journal of Control Systems*, vol. 2, pp. 464–476, 2023, doi:10.1109/OJCSYS.2023.3324274
  (open access, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). The Chapter 16 notebook recomputes its
  results; it does not copy the paper's figures.
- **Prof. T. Charalambous.** The author's introductory lectures also drew on his slides.

All code, plots and exercise text in this repository were written for these notes. The
mathematical models used (first-order lags, double integrators, DC motors and so on) are standard
in the field.
